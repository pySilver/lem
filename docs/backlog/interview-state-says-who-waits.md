# The interview's state says who it waits on

A task with an interview writes one state, `held` with `the interview waits for the owner`, before
its first question (`stages/new.md`, step 4), and the next state comes after his yes to the
criteria. In between, the scout reads, the personas read the criteria, and Lem writes them, and
the board and `lem project waiting` say all that time that the task waits for him (the board's
head "The interview waits for you", `now_head`).

The case, 2026-11-19 in Lem's own repository: a second Lem session ran the interviews of
`landing-in-the-background` and `interview-in-a-companion`. At 17:03 local time the personas read
the criteria of the first, and the owner had nothing to answer; the board listed both under what
waits for him. He wrote: "I'm lost. I see that landing task is waiting for me and I cannot reach
it's question anywhere", and later "board seems to be bullshitting me".

What is missing: who acts now. A `held` block while the read or the scout runs, with a line that
says so (`the personas read the criteria`), and `held` with the owner's line again when a question
or the page waits for him. `lem project waiting` and the board's "Waits for you" then list the
task only while he is the one. Where he answers is the task's own session (`lem session`), which
the board opens with "Open a session".
