# A task order in his words stays

The order of tasks, from the retro of 2026-11-21:

**A task order in his words stays** (2026-11-21). On 2026-11-20 the ask of the task
`tests-at-the-unit-level` began "After one-lem-parallel-landings: ...". Lem started its lead at
once, the lead decided "it goes on now, with no after: line", since the task needed no code of
the other one, and he overturned it three minutes later: "it waits" (his word as the retro
carried it). The retro of 2026-11-21 proposed, as `owner-order-stays`: a task also waits when the
owner's words put it after another task ("after X", "when X lands"); an order that he gave
stays, a lead never removes it, and a lead that sees no need for the order says so in its
report. A session told him that two proposals of that retro still stood, and he typed "im dnd,
go alone - register tasks via /lem": his word to make the proposal a task, and no more.
Everything else is the session's design, taken while he was away, and open to him: the first
line of the `queued` block, `the owner's order: "HIS WORDS"`, which tells his order from a need,
with no script that reads an ask's words; the line `then: PATH`, where a task queued in the
shape goes on when its wait ends; for words that name no task Lem can tell, one question with
three answers ("Queue this after SLUG, after another task (name it), or no order?"), and while
he is away the one open task that fits, or the hold `the order waits for the owner` when none
or several fit; his "start SLUG now", which removes his order; and the board server's wake of
the live session of a queued task whose wait ended, with a close that gives up on a session
woken after it started. He did not approve the acceptance criteria, and no level of the ladder
settled where a queued task goes on or what Lem does with an order it cannot read: the lead's
pick stands for both (Decisions 3 and 7 of `docs/plans/2026-11-21-owner-task-order-stays.md`).
This replaces the word "only" in the maintaining session's decision
[An ask waits for another task when it needs that task's result, not when it names the same code](../../.lem/decisions/an-ask-waits-for-another-task-only-when-it-needs-that-tasks-result.md)
(2026-11-17), whose other half stays: two asks that only name the same file or function each
start at once.
