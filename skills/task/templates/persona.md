# NAME, in two or three words (who this person is)

One person, not a segment. A panel asks this person what they would see and do, so write what
lets a reader answer as them.

## Who they are

Their role, how often they meet the product, how much they know about how it works.

## What they want

What they come for, and what would make them leave or complain.

## What they notice

What is on their screen or in their inbox: the things a change can make better or worse for them.

## What they never see

The internals that mean nothing to them. An answer in these terms is not theirs.
