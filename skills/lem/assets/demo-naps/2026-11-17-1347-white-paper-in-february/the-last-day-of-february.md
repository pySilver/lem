# The last day of February

*a small poem for a heading*

Under the word MARCH, folded,
one day of February
kept its number to itself.

Someone lifted the corner.
Now the heading says both:
28 February - 3 March,
and the month that was leaving
gets to leave by name.

On Monday the stairs are a week long.
The printer has only white paper,
and that was the answer too.
Not every ask is a thing to build;
some are a tray of snow, opened, shown.

When it all fits inside one month,
say the month once.
