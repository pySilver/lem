# Dream

There is a long table, and on it a calendar that has been folded so that the end of February
and the start of March touch, like two hands. Someone has written MARCH across the fold in
large letters, and the last two days of February are under it, still there, but nobody can
read them. I keep lifting the corner to check. They are there. 28. 1. 2. 3. February had only
the one day left in it and it was holding on.

A printer in the corner is printing invoices, and every one comes out black. Black paper, black
ink, and the numbers on them are visible only if you tilt them toward the window. A customer
stands beside it waiting. They say, quite kindly, "but I asked for white." The printer says
there is no other kind of paper. It opens its tray to prove it, and the tray is full of snow.

Then it is Monday, and I am carrying a box of orders up a staircase. Nine thousand of them.
I cannot put the first one down until I have carried all of them to the top. Tuesday walks
past me with two orders in its pocket and goes straight in. I don't mind. The staircase is
the whole week, and I know that now.

At the top, someone has fixed the calendar. It says 28 February - 3 March, and below it, on
a small card, 3 - 9 March, with the month only once, because that is how the shop writes it.
The red light by the door went off and then on in green. Nobody said anything. I put the box
down.
