# PROJECT NAME

Every persona, reviewer and builder reads this page first. Keep it to one page: what a newcomer
would get wrong without it.

## What it is

Two or three sentences: the product, who it is for, where it runs.

## Scale

What it handles today and what it is designed for. A design that works at today's size and fails
at the designed size is wrong now.

## Rules every answer works within

The few constraints that outrank a clever idea: what is never compromised, what compatibility is
or is not owed, what "done" means here.

## Tools and versions

The stack, with versions, so a claim about a tool's behavior can be checked against the right
release.

## What a newcomer gets wrong

The assumptions that are true in most projects and false in this one.

## Who to compare with

The teams or products whose public practice is a fair comparison, and the ones that are not
because of their size.
