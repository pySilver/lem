# Lessons

A correction that the owner gave and that has no file of its own yet. `/lem learn` moves a lesson
into the file that owns its step when the same correction comes back.

interview: a guess that adds a mechanism to Lem (a guard, a lock, a record, a queue, a check) names the act of Lem's that does harm without it and its cost on a small task; a guess that cannot name both is left out (`docs/rulings/rulings.md`, "Lem is a companion, not a gate").
learn: in this repository the groups of the rulings list are the twelve that `tests/unit/test_agents_md.py` names in `GROUPS`, so a ruling goes under one of them and the retro adds no heading, and "Choices of the maintaining session" stays the list's last part.
lem-gate: in this repository `docs/rulings/history.md` stands beside the list and holds rulings still in force, each with its test or its place; a bullet under "Choices of the maintaining session", and a history line whose day says `decided`, is the session's choice and not the owner's ruling.
